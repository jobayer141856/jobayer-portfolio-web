# Open Product Questions

Resolve these with the portfolio owner before final visual design and implementation. Do not invent answers.

## Brand and audience
- [ ] Confirm primary audience: recruiters, engineering teams, academic contacts, clients, or all.
- [ ] Confirm final professional headline.
- [ ] Confirm preferred visual direction: minimal light, modern dark, editorial, or creative.
- [ ] Confirm light theme, dark theme, or both.
- [ ] Confirm brand/accent color and typography preferences.
- [ ] Confirm English only or bilingual English/Bangla.

## Content
- [ ] Approve the final list of public sections.
- [ ] Confirm whether blog is part of the first release.
- [ ] Confirm whether a profile photo will be used.
- [ ] Confirm which résumé file/version may be published.
- [ ] Confirm whether email address should be displayed publicly.
- [ ] Approve public social profile links.
- [ ] Approve biography, experience, project, education, and publication content.
- [ ] Confirm whether academic result/CGPA should be public.

## Functionality
- [ ] Confirm contact form fields.
- [ ] Confirm whether project filters are needed.
- [ ] Confirm whether a theme switch is required.
- [ ] Confirm whether a dedicated skills page is needed or a home-page section is sufficient.
- [ ] Confirm whether blog categories/search are required if blog is enabled.

## Technical and operations
- [ ] Confirm Angular version when implementation begins.
- [ ] Confirm API base URL and OpenAPI contract.
- [ ] Confirm SSR/prerendering strategy.
- [ ] Confirm hosting provider and domain.
- [ ] Confirm analytics and privacy requirements.
- [ ] Confirm image/file delivery strategy with backend.

## Approval
- [ ] Product owner approves sitemap.
- [ ] Product owner approves visual direction.
- [ ] Product owner approves public content.
- [ ] Product owner approves first-release scope.
